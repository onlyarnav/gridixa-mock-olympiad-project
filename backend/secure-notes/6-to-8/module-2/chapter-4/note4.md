# What is a Prompt?

A **prompt** is the instruction or text that you give to an AI system to tell it **what task you want it to perform**.

In simple terms:

> A **prompt is the question or instruction you type into an AI**.

For example, when you ask a question to systems like:

* ChatGPT
* Gemini

the text you type is the **prompt**.

The AI reads your prompt and generates a response based on it.

---

# Why Prompts Matter

Large Language Models are extremely powerful, but they rely on **clear instructions**.

The **quality of the prompt directly affects the quality of the response**.

A vague prompt often produces **generic or unclear answers**, while a detailed prompt produces **better and more useful results**.

---

# Prompt Engineering

Because prompting is so important, a new field in technology has emerged called **Prompt Engineering**.

Prompt Engineering is the practice of **designing clear, structured prompts that guide AI to produce the best possible output**.

Professionals in this field specialize in:

* Writing detailed instructions for AI systems
* Structuring prompts for better results
* Testing and improving prompts

This skill is becoming increasingly important as AI tools become more common in industries such as marketing, education, software development, and design.

---

# A Helpful Analogy

Think of prompting like giving a **creative brief to a marketing team**.

If you give very little information, the team has to **guess what you want**.

But if you give clear instructions, the team can create something much better.

---

# Example of a Bad Prompt

Bad Prompt:

> “Design a poster for a youth event.”

This instruction is too vague.

The AI does not know:

* What style to use
* Who the audience is
* What tone to follow
* What message the poster should contain

Because of this, the result will likely be **generic and uninteresting**.

---

# Example of a Great Prompt

Great Prompt:

> “Act as an expert graphic designer. Design a highly detailed, minimalist promotional poster for an event called ‘The Youth Talks.’ Use bold typography, modern branding, and a serious tone aimed at Gen Z students. Include a short tagline about the future of education.”

This prompt works much better because it clearly provides:

* **A role** (expert graphic designer)
* **A task** (design a promotional poster)
* **A style** (minimalist, bold typography)
* **A target audience** (Gen Z students)
* **A message** (future of education)

With this information, the AI can produce a **much higher-quality result**.

---

# Key Elements of a Good Prompt

Good prompts usually include several helpful details.

**Role**

Tell the AI what role it should take.

Example: “Act as a teacher,” “Act as a marketing expert.”

**Task**

Clearly describe what you want the AI to do.

Example: write, summarize, design, explain.

**Context**

Provide background information so the AI understands the situation.

**Style or Tone**

Specify how the response should sound.

Example: professional, friendly, serious, creative.

# Think Like an AI Engineer

Consider the following two prompts.

Prompt A:
“Write about climate change.”

Prompt B:
“Act as an environmental scientist. Write a short article explaining climate change to middle-school students using simple language and real-world examples.”

Which prompt will produce a better result?

A) Prompt A
B) Prompt B

Correct answer: **B**

Prompt B gives the AI **clear instructions, audience, and context**, which leads to a better response.
