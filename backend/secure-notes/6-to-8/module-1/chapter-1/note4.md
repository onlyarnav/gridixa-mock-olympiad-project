# 1950: The Turing Test

In 1950, a brilliant mathematician named Alan Turing asked a very important question:

> **Can machines think?**

To explore this idea, he designed an experiment called the **Turing Test**.

### How the Turing Test Works

The idea is simple.

A human has a **text conversation** with two hidden participants:

* One is **another human**
* One is **a machine**

The human asking questions does not know which is which.

The process can be represented like this:

```id="ai10"
Human Judge → Text Conversation → Hidden Participants (Human + Machine) → Judge Makes Guess
```

If the human judge **cannot tell the difference** between the machine and the human, the machine is considered to have **passed the Turing Test**.

Passing the test suggests that the machine can **behave intelligently in conversation**.

This idea became one of the earliest ways scientists thought about **machine intelligence**.

# 1997: Deep Blue vs. Garry Kasparov

Chess is a game known for its **deep strategy and planning**.

In 1997, a powerful computer named **Deep Blue** competed against the world chess champion **Garry Kasparov**.

The computer was developed by the company IBM.

The match was historic.

For the first time, a computer **defeated the human world champion in a chess match**.

### How the AI Played Chess

The computer analyzed huge numbers of possible moves.

```id="ai11"
Current Board → Analyze Millions of Moves → Predict Best Outcome → Choose Move
```

This moment surprised many people.

It showed that machines could **analyze complex strategies and outperform human reasoning in certain tasks**.

---

# 2012: The Deep Learning Breakthrough

For many years, computers struggled to understand **images and photographs**.

If you showed a computer a picture of a dog, it could not easily recognize what it was seeing.

Then in 2012, scientists made a major breakthrough using a method called **Deep Learning**.

Deep learning allows computers to **learn patterns from extremely large amounts of data**.

### How Deep Learning Works for Images

Scientists trained computers with **millions of images**.

The AI studied patterns such as:

* Shapes
* Colors
* Edges
* Object structures

The learning process looked like this:

```id="ai12"
Large Image Dataset → Pattern Learning → Feature Detection → Object Recognition
```

After training, computers could recognize:

* Faces
* Animals
* Everyday objects

This breakthrough greatly improved technologies such as:

* Image recognition
* Photo organization
* Self-driving systems

---

# 2017: The Introduction of Transformers

In 2017, researchers at the company Google introduced a new type of AI design called **Transformers**.

Despite the name, these are **not robot cars**. They are a special type of **AI architecture** designed to understand language.

Transformers made it much easier for AI systems to understand:

* Words
* Grammar
* Sentence meaning
* Context in conversations

### How Transformer Models Process Language

```id="ai13"
Text Input → Context Analysis → Language Understanding → Intelligent Response
```

This innovation allowed computers to understand human language **far better than before**.

It eventually led to the development of advanced systems such as modern **AI chatbots and language assistants**.
