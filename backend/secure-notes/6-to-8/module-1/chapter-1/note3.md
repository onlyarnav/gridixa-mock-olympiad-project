# Understanding AI Categories

Researchers divide Artificial Intelligence into two major types:

1. **Narrow AI (Weak AI)**
2. **General AI (Strong AI)**

These categories help us understand **what AI systems can and cannot do**.

Let us explore each type carefully.

---

# Type 1: Narrow AI (Weak AI)

**Narrow AI** is the **only type of AI that currently exists in the real world.**

A Narrow AI system is **very intelligent at one specific task**, but it cannot perform tasks outside its area.

It is like a specialist who is extremely good at **one job**, but not trained for other jobs.

### Key Idea

> Narrow AI focuses on **one specific problem**.

Even though it is limited to one task, it can perform that task **extremely well**.

---

# Example: Ball Tracking in Cricket

In cricket matches, a technology called **Decision Review System (DRS)** is used.

One part of this system tracks the **movement of the cricket ball** and predicts its path.

This AI system studies:

* Ball speed
* Ball direction
* Pitch bounce
* Angle of movement

Using this information, it predicts:

**Would the ball have hit the stumps?**

The process can be represented like this:

```id="ai5"
Ball Data → AI Physics Analysis → Path Prediction → Decision Support for Umpire
```

The system is extremely good at **predicting ball movement**.

However, imagine asking this AI system to:

* Tell a joke
* Write an essay
* Play music

It would not be able to do any of these things.

Why?

Because it was designed **only for one task**.

This is why it is called **Narrow AI**.

---

# Why It Is Called “Narrow”

The word **narrow** means **limited or focused on one area**.

A Narrow AI system works like this:

```id="ai6"
One Task → Deep Expertise → No Ability Outside That Task
```

Examples of Narrow AI systems include:

* Voice assistants
* Recommendation systems
* Face recognition systems
* Game-playing AI

Each one is designed for **a specific purpose**.

---

# Type 2: General AI (Strong AI)

The second category is called **General AI**.

General AI refers to a machine that can **think and learn like a human across many different tasks**.

A General AI system would be able to:

* Learn new skills
* Understand different subjects
* Solve many types of problems
* Communicate naturally with humans

### Key Idea

> General AI would have **human-level intelligence across all activities**.

It would not be limited to just one task.

---

# Example from Fiction

To imagine General AI, think about a magical object that can **understand people, reason, and communicate**.

A well-known example is the **Sorting Hat** from the book *Harry Potter* by J. K. Rowling.

The hat can:

* Look into a student's mind
* Understand their personality
* Hold a conversation
* Make a complex decision

This type of flexible thinking is similar to what **General AI would be capable of**.

The reasoning process could look like this:

```id="ai7"
Observe Person → Understand Personality → Reason About Traits → Make Decision
```

This is very different from Narrow AI, which focuses on only **one specific task**.

---

# Important Fact

Even though scientists are researching General AI, it **does not exist yet**.

Today, General AI can only be found in:

* Science fiction stories
* Movies
* Fantasy books

Researchers around the world are still trying to understand **how such an intelligent system could be built**.
