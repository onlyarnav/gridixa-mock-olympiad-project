# Smart Assistants

Many smartphones and smart speakers include **voice assistants** that can respond to your commands.

Examples include:

* Siri
* Amazon Alexa
* Google Assistant

These assistants use Artificial Intelligence to:

* Understand your voice
* Convert speech into text
* Interpret what you mean
* Perform the requested action

The process works like this:

```id="ai14"
Voice Command → Speech Recognition → Meaning Understanding → Action Performed
```

For example, you might say:

* “Set an alarm for 7 AM.”
* “What is the capital of Japan?”
* “Play some music.”

The AI assistant understands the request and performs the task.

# Navigation and Traffic Prediction

Navigation apps help people travel by suggesting **the fastest routes**.

A well-known example is Google Maps.

This system uses Artificial Intelligence to study traffic patterns.

The AI analyzes information such as:

* Speed of vehicles on different roads
* Traffic conditions
* Road congestion

One interesting method is that the system can analyze the **movement speed of thousands of mobile phones on the road**.

Using this information, the AI predicts where traffic is slow and suggests better routes.

The process can be represented like this:

```id="ai15"
Traffic Data → Pattern Analysis → Traffic Prediction → Fastest Route Suggestion
```

This helps users reach their destination **more quickly and efficiently**.

# Chatbots on Websites

When you visit many websites, you may see a **small chat window** appear in the corner.

This chat box may offer help such as:

* Finding a product
* Answering questions
* Providing support

In many cases, the assistant responding to you is **not a human**, but an AI system called a **chatbot**.

Chatbots use AI to:

* Understand typed questions
* Find relevant information
* Generate helpful responses

The chatbot interaction process works like this:

```id="ai16"
User Question → Language Understanding → Information Search → AI Response
```

Businesses use chatbots because they can **assist many customers at the same time**.

# Self-Driving Cars

One of the most advanced uses of Artificial Intelligence is in **self-driving vehicles**.

Some companies are developing cars that can **drive with little or no human control**.

An example is the technology developed by Tesla.

These vehicles use AI together with hardware systems such as:

* Cameras
* Sensors
* Radar systems

These devices act like **“AI eyes”** that constantly observe the environment.

The system must recognize many things on the road, including:

* Stop signs
* Traffic lights
* Pedestrians
* Other vehicles

The AI system processes this information like this:

```id="ai17"
Camera and Sensor Data → Object Detection → Road Understanding → Driving Decision
```

Based on what it detects, the AI decides:

* When to slow down
* When to stop
* When to turn
* When it is safe to move forward

This allows the vehicle to **navigate safely on the road**.
