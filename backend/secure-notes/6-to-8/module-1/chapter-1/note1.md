# Step 1: What Makes Humans Intelligent?

First, think about what humans can do.

Humans can:

* **Learn** from experience
* **Understand language**
* **Recognize patterns**
* **Solve problems**
* **Learn from mistakes**

### Example

If you see these two animals:

Dog
Cat

Even if the dog is big or small, brown or black, you can still recognize that it is a **dog**.

This ability is called:

**Pattern Recognition**

Your brain automatically learns patterns over time.

# Think and Observe

### Question

How do you know the difference between a **dog** and a **cat**?

Possible clues:

* Shape of ears
* Size
* Sound they make
* Tail shape

Your brain has learned these patterns by observing them many times.

This is an example of **human intelligence**.

# Step 2: Can Machines Become Intelligent?

Now let us think about a **computer**.

A normal computer usually **follows strict instructions**.

For example:

### Calculator Example

If you type:

```
5 + 5
```

The calculator returns:

```
10
```

However, there is something important to understand.

The calculator:

* Does **not know what the number 5 means**
* Cannot **learn new things**
* Cannot **recognize patterns**
* Cannot **make decisions**

It simply **follows rules written by a programmer**.

---

# The Big Idea: Artificial Intelligence

### Definition

> **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is the science of giving computers the ability to **learn, think, and make decisions like humans.**

Instead of only following rules, AI systems:

1. Take in information
2. Learn from that information
3. Improve over time
4. Make their own decisions

---

# Imagine an AI Example

### Recognizing Animals

Suppose we show a computer **1000 pictures of dogs** and **1000 pictures of cats**.

The AI studies these images and learns patterns such as:

Dogs may have:

* Longer snouts
* Different ear shapes

Cats may have:

* Smaller faces
* Pointed ears

After learning these patterns, the AI can **predict new images correctly**.

Example:

New Image → AI Prediction → “This is a Dog”

This process is how AI learns from data.

### AI System


Both involve a **learning process**.
